Fall-risk screen comparison

300 participants · 125 fallers in 12 months (42%) · published cutoffs vs a sensitivity-first cutoff (specificity ≥ 0.50)

Key findings
  • Highest AUC: New tool (0.75, 95% CI 0.69–0.80).
  • Highest sensitivity with specificity ≥ 0.50: New tool at >= 7 (sensitivity 0.84, specificity 0.54).
  • New tool: published cutoff sensitivity 0.76, specificity 0.64, LR− 0.38.
  • Berg Balance Scale: published cutoff sensitivity 0.72, specificity 0.63, LR− 0.45.
  • Tinetti POMA: published cutoff sensitivity 0.51, specificity 0.73, LR− 0.67.
  • SPPB: published cutoff sensitivity 0.86, specificity 0.27, LR− 0.50.

These AUC CIs are unpaired bootstrap intervals. To claim one tool beats another, use a paired test such as DeLong's.
