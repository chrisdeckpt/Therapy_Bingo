# Results

**Results:** 20 participants completed 3 trials of knee-wrap application at each pressure condition. At the low limb occlusion pressure condition (mean 70.3 ± 10.1 mmHg), intrarater reliability was good for a single trial (ICC(3,1) = 0.88, 95% CI 0.77–0.95) and excellent for the mean of 3 trials (ICC(3,3) = 0.96, 95% CI 0.91–0.98). The SEM was 3.5 mmHg and the MDC95 was 9.7 mmHg. At the high limb occlusion pressure condition (mean 142.4 ± 16.0 mmHg), intrarater reliability was excellent for a single trial (ICC(3,1) = 0.92, 95% CI 0.84–0.97) and excellent for the mean of 3 trials (ICC(3,3) = 0.97, 95% CI 0.94–0.99). The SEM was 4.5 mmHg and the MDC95 was 12.4 mmHg.

Bland-Altman analysis: low condition: largest mean bias +2.0 mmHg (T1-T3), limits of agreement across trial pairs ranged from -9.7 to 12.0 mmHg; high condition: largest mean bias -0.7 mmHg (T1-T3), limits of agreement across trial pairs ranged from -14.2 to 13.3 mmHg. No pair of trials showed significant proportional bias (regression of difference on mean, all p ≥ 0.05).
